          Approve Offer

          WARNING!

          Once you approve the Offer, you will no longer be able to edit it, and you will lose all control over it. The Offer will be autonomously managed solely by its own algorithms.

          From the moment of approval, the Offer will be open for collecting contributions and conducting voting until it is either successfully completed or ends unsuccessfully.

          When the Offer is successfully completed, all funds contributed by participants will be automatically transferred to the contender who wins the vote, according to the parameters set for this Offer.

          Approval cannot be undone. This is your last chance to cancel the operation if you are unsure that everything is in order.

          Cancel Contribution
